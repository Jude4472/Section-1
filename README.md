# Section-1

An Undertale-inspired browser-based boss battle game.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to move your soul during combat
3. Choose your action:
   - **ATTACK** - Deal damage to the boss
   - **ACT** - Try to befriend or distract the boss
   - **ITEM** - Use an item to heal
   - **MERCY** - Offer to spare the boss (requires 2 acts of mercy)

## Features

- Turn-based battle system inspired by Undertale
- Dodge dangerous bullet patterns
- HP system for both player and boss
- Multiple attack patterns from the boss
- Mercy mechanic to win without fighting
- Score tracking with turn counter
- Particle effects and visual feedback

## Game Modes

- **Select Mode**: Choose your action (Attack, Act, Item, Mercy)
- **Dodge Mode**: Navigate bullet patterns
- **Win/Lose**: Battle outcomes

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** - Move
- **Mouse** - Click buttons
- **Space** - Skip dodge phase (if battle is active)

Enjoy the battle!
